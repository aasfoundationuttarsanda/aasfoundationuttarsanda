About Us

The Journey of AAS Foundation Uttarsanda

For more than 15 years, our journey began as a quiet, close-knit initiative dedicated to providing monthly Ration Kits to widowed mothers and their families. Driven by a deep commitment to service, our Respected President envisioned creating an enduring institutional umbrella to amplify this vital work.

On July 6, 2023, AAS Foundation was formally registered. What started as humble grassroots relief has blossomed, through the steady dedication of dear friends, family, and compassionate well-wishers, into a mighty, ever-expanding banyan tree—nurturing countless lives every single day.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive, equitable, and flourishing society where every individual enjoys equal opportunities for quality education, economic self-reliance, and dignity.

Our Mission

Through strategic interventions in progressive education, economic enablement, holistic health, sustainable environmental practices, and social equality, we foster an inclusive society with equitable opportunities for all members of the human family.

What We Do

Educational Empowerment

Making Learning Engaging, Fun & Accessible

  • Direct sponsorship of school admission and examination fees.
  • Remedial learning support producing noticeable marksheet improvements.
  • Merit scholarships for professional higher education studies.
  • Activity-based workshops to teach science and arts with joy.
"Change education in a fun way and change society."
Family & Nutrition Support

Monthly Grocery & Ration Kits

Kit Ingredients: Mug, Chana Dal, Toor Dal, Sugar, Chilli Powder, Turmeric Powder, and Pouch of Oil.
  • Essential staple food kit supporting kitchen nutrition directly.
  • Prioritized for single mothers and widows with minor or schooling children.
  • Regular doorstep delivery to safeguard family dignity.
  • Relieving kitchen distress so children can focus fully on studies.
Verified monthly distribution records maintained with zero leakage.
